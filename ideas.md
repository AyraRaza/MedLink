# MedLink Design Direction

## Ground-truth reference
The attached product brief is the visual source of truth: warm off-white canvas, black vertical navigation, oversized rounded white cards, restrained pastel accents, soft shadows, compact line icons, generous whitespace, and a premium healthcare-technology dashboard feel. The prototype should preserve this language without copying a specific layout mechanically. The product is an internal biomedical resource and inventory platform for verified healthcare organizations; marketplace, exchange, payments, and transaction history remain out of scope except as clearly labeled future placeholders.

## Style Decisions

### Design Movement
Quiet luxury SaaS: editorial Swiss structure softened with warm industrial materials and healthcare calm.

### Core Principles
1. **Monochrome first.** Black, charcoal, cream, and white carry the interface; color is reserved for status, emphasis, and orientation.
2. **Soft utility.** Every rounded surface should make dense operational information feel less intimidating without becoming playful.
3. **Asymmetric balance.** Use a strong left rail, uneven content proportions, and one dominant visual panel rather than a generic centered admin grid.
4. **Confidence through restraint.** Keep hierarchy clear, motion subtle, and status colors muted enough for clinical credibility.

### Color Philosophy
The warm off-white background keeps long sessions comfortable, while near-black navigation gives the product an unmistakable operational spine. Pastel lavender, mint, peach, and blue-gray accents act as quiet signals rather than decoration: lavender for overview, mint for verified/available, peach for attention, and blue-gray for neutral metadata. Signature brand color: **MedLink Lilac `#C8B8F2`**.

### Layout Paradigm
A persistent black rail anchors the experience. Content opens into a loose 45/55 dashboard composition: recent operational records on the left, a larger organization/resource overview panel on the right. Supporting pages retain the same shell but shift to focused cards and breathable forms. On mobile, navigation becomes a bottom rail and cards stack with deliberate priority.

### Signature Elements
- A compact symbol combining a linked medical cross and inventory crate, rendered as a bold white mark in the rail.
- A lilac-to-blush “network pulse” overview card with small circular nodes suggesting connected healthcare organizations without implying live marketplace activity.
- Status pills with soft tinted backgrounds and dark text, never alarmist traffic-light colors.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like moving through a calm operations room: direct, reversible, and informative. Sidebar items, cards, filters, and resource rows are clickable; actions surface concise success or placeholder feedback. Hover states lift surfaces slightly, while focus states remain visible and accessible.

### Animation
Use 160–220ms ease-out transitions for buttons, nav tiles, chips, and cards. Page content can fade and translate upward by 4px on entry. Avoid looping motion and decorative parallax. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use **Manrope** for headings and **Inter** for interface copy, with 700/800 display weights and 400/500 body weights. Headings are compact and slightly tracked; labels use uppercase microcopy with generous letter spacing. Numbers are large and tabular-looking for scanability.

### Brand Essence
MedLink is the calm operating layer for verified healthcare organizations managing biomedical resources, built for teams that need clarity before complexity. Personality: **assured, precise, humane**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and quietly optimistic. CTAs describe the action, not a vague promise. Microcopy reassures without minimizing operational importance.

Example lines:
- “Keep critical resources in view.”
- “Your organization is verified and ready to manage inventory.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo mark is a bold geometric link-cross: two interlocking rounded bars form a medical cross in negative space, paired with a custom uppercase MEDLINK wordmark using tightened Manrope geometry. The prototype uses the symbol as a standalone rail mark and favicon-scale visual.

### Component reminders
Every component should ask: **Does this choice reinforce or dilute the quiet-luxury SaaS direction?**
